The route

Copenhagen → Stockholm, chapter by chapter.

Chapter 1

North Zealand & the Øresund

km 0–63

Road: past Fredensborg Palace (km 40) to Kronborg in Helsingør. Gravel: through Jægersborg Dyrehave, past Frederiksborg Castle (km 46), Gribskov forest and Esrum Lake, out to Hornbæk, then Kronborg (km 82). Both: twenty minutes across the Øresund to Helsingborg.

    Chapter 2

    Skåne & Söderåsen

    km 63–175

    Bjuv, Kågeröd and Ljungbyhed at the foot of the Söderåsen ridge, a national park of deep ravines and beech forest. Perstorp, then Hässleholm (road) or Bjärnum and Hästveda (gravel), to Osby.

      Chapter 3

      IKEA country & the lakes

      km 175–300

      Älmhult (road km 204), where IKEA began, Lönsboda and Vislanda, to the lakeside cathedral city of Växjö (km 299 / 314).

        Chapter 4

        The Småland highlands

        km 300–450

        Rottne, Braås, Åseda, Virserum, Mariannelund and Hultsfred. Forest, lakes and red farmhouses, and the roof of the route at around 280–295 m (km 355 road, km 375 gravel).

          Chapter 5

          Vimmerby to the Baltic

          km 450–600

          Vimmerby (km 457 / 455), Astrid Lindgren's hometown, then Södra Vi, Åtvidaberg and a long forest stretch to Söderköping and Mem (km 594 / 591), where the Göta Canal meets the Baltic.

            Chapter 6

            Bråviken & the Sörmland coast

            km 600–720

            Across the Vikbolandet peninsula to the free Skenäs ferry over the Bråviken inlet (km 621 / 617), Stigtomta, then Nyköping and its castle (km 655 / 651), Oxelösund, Trosa (road), known as "the end of the world", and Tullgarn royal palace nearby.

              Chapter 7

              Into Stockholm

              km 720–784

              Across the island of Mörkö and the three-minute Skanssund ferry to Hörningsnäs (km 734 / 728), then Pershagen, Tullinge and Huddinge, through Årsta and over the water into central Stockholm.
