## Good to know

- Three ferries, all free for Nordic Chase riders: Helsingør–Helsingborg (every 15–30 minutes, day and night, free through our ferry partnership), then two short Swedish road ferries: Skenäsleden across Bråviken (km 621 road, km 617 gravel) and Skanssundsleden from Mörkö to Hörningsnäs (km 734 road, km 728 gravel). The two Swedish ferries usually run from around 05:30 in the morning until the evening, not through the night. Timetables can change, so check the latest times for [Skenäsleden](https://www.trafikverket.se/resa-och-trafik/farjetrafik/skenasleden/) and [Skanssundsleden](https://www.trafikverket.se/resa-och-trafik/farjetrafik/skanssundsleden/) before you plan your night.
- Sweden's right of public access means you can camp almost anywhere for a night (see Sleeping on the road).
- Between Växjö and Söderköping towns are small and far apart: refill when you can.
